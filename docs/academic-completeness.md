# Academic completeness — presentation audit

Reviewed locally on **2026-11-12** using the already collected official LIU sources. No new student, enrollment, payment or grade records were created.

## Scope and meaning of complete

- All five schools, 65 program entries and three Freshman pathways remain visible.
- All 65 program entries retain published graduation credits and now have locally stored course tables.
- 2,069 **program-course entries** are displayed. These are not 2,069 unique courses; shared courses recur in different degree requirements.
- 53 programs have complete **credit-reconciled semester plans**: every displayed term subtotal equals its course credits and all terms sum to the published graduation requirement.
- 37 concise course-description summaries are verified for Computer Engineering. Other detailed descriptions remain unavailable. Consequently, **no program is claimed to have every requested syllabus/detail field fully complete**.
- Three Freshman pathways retain the published minimum of 30 credits; detailed course/semester plans are not available locally.
- Public tuition, additional fees, campus contacts and admission requirements are restored from the existing research. Rates are subject to change. Public data never creates portal records.

## Internal evidence and reproducibility

- Program/credit provenance: `research-programs.json` and `public-sources.md`.
- 193 existing linked PDFs were read and retained in the ignored `tmp/pdfs/` cache.
- `academic-document-audit.json` records document URLs and raw extraction evidence. Some raw font streams are encoded; the compiler resolves embedded Unicode maps when extracting cells.
- `curriculum-extraction-review.json` records per-document course counts, unparsed pages and plan subtotal reconciliation.
- `course-description-summaries.json` holds concise paraphrases checked against the Computer Engineering course-description document.
- `scripts/compile-curricula.py` compiles only local, previously collected documents. No backend runs and no external links are needed by the public academic pages.

## Known source cautions (internal only)

Legacy graduate listings require intake confirmation. The older 100-credit CS/IT catalog differs from current 99-credit material; the current tables are used. Some diploma contract headers say 24 while rows and current program tables total 28; no header is silently substituted for the verified graduation requirement. Biomedical degree naming differs between some documents. Cohort applicability and descriptions not independently interpreted remain subject to registrar review. Official public fees differ from the separate registration application's four-installment/$20 requirements; those application rules remain unchanged.
